# Roadmap

This roadmap communicates current direction, not a delivery promise. Priorities
can change as the learning content and community evolve.

## Completed

- M0–M10: foundational product, scenario, validation, and learning-experience
  milestones are complete.

## Current

- M11: community release preparation, including contribution, conduct, security,
  issue, and pull-request guidance.

## Exploring

- M12: an optional AI extension contract: a provider-agnostic future AI
  adapter/relay design that can preserve the core experience without making AI
  functionality or an adapter a requirement. This is a noncommitment, not an
  integration promise.

Future ideas are noncommitments, not promises of scope, timing, or support.

# Scoring rubric

Milestone 6 scores a submitted diagnosis deterministically in the browser:

- **70 points:** the submitted diagnosis matches the scenario's authored diagnosis.
- **30 points:** the share of unique critical evidence items the learner bookmarked,
  rounded to the nearest whole point.

The result is a safe summary: submitted diagnosis, total score, and a broad
outcome. It does not disclose an answer key, evidence relevance labels, a score
breakdown, or an explanation. Scores are in-memory only and reset when the
investigation restarts or the page refreshes.

Remediation, communication, efficiency, detailed breakdowns, explanations, and
debrief are not yet scored.
